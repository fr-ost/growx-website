# Product requirements (current, owner-approved)

1. **Legal pages:** Privacy Policy and Terms of Service exist as clearly marked drafts requiring legal review before public launch. They use only supplied business details (name: Shahriar Ahmed Tushar; Rajshahi, Bangladesh) and make no unsupported legal claims; governing law, retention and refund terms remain placeholders.
2. **Free/Premium:** see `FEATURE_SPLIT.md`. Freemium, 14-day trial, no grandfathering, trial requires a confirmed email and an X username.
3. **Past-due payments:** configurable grace period (`BILLING_GRACE_DAYS_CARD`, default **3**) for failed *recurring card* payments, applied only if the provider supports a retry/dunning window. During grace Premium stays on and the dashboard/account show a payment warning; afterwards the user reverts to Free unless a verified successful payment restores access. Lifetime purchases are unaffected. Crypto renewal failures use `BILLING_GRACE_DAYS_CRYPTO` (default **0**): they follow verified provider events and the policy in `PAYMENTS.md`, not card-retry behaviour.
4. **Early-adopter offer:** first 100 successful, verified $0.99 lifetime purchases get permanent Premium. Slots are allocated atomically in the database (`claim_early_adopter_slot`). Failed/canceled/abandoned checkouts never count. When sold out the offer is hidden and the regular prices ($1.99/mo, $14.99/yr, $29.99 lifetime) apply. No remaining-slot count is shown. Until checkout exists the offer is shown as planned and cannot be bought.
5. **Providers:** development continues without Paddle/NOWPayments approval; only placeholders and (later) sandbox integrations. Live payments are not claimed until credentials, verification and end-to-end tests are complete. **Live payment processing is out of scope for this phase.**
6. Support email: support@growxapp.net.
